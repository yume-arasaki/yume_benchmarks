# RTX 4090 drag race on Qwen 3.8 27B

2026-09-15 · [@yume_arasaki](https://x.com/yume_arasaki)

My 4090 turns four next month. I wanted to see how far it could go on Qwen 3.8 27B, so I treated it like a drag racing car: strip it, tune it, point it down the strip.

The tune is [syv-ai/qwen38-27b-rtx3090](https://github.com/syv-ai/qwen38-27b-rtx3090) (ledwardchow's build). Built for the slower 3090, where it reads 127-133 tok/s single-stream. I ported it as-is: vLLM 0.27.1 with his 13 patches applied into site-packages, W4A16 AutoRound base plus his fast variant (calibrated int4 GPTQ lm_head and drafter), DFlash2 block drafter at k=7, lookup drafting on, CTX=fast (bf16 KV, 64k window). His recipe, my card. Full credit to him.

Tok/s is decode after first token unless marked e2e. Quality gated with his battery, because a fast server that spits garbage is worthless.

## The headline run

His own harness (`vllm bench serve`, 8 real prompts, 1024 tokens out, single stream, run twice, second kept as he specifies):

## The quality gate

His battery, run as shipped: GSM8K **95.5%** (n=200), perplexity en 10.77 / code 3.06 / all 8.09. Matches his own 95-97%. Speed did not cost quality.

## The ladder

His documented optimization steps on the 3090, my run bolted on the end:

46 (no speculation) → 66 (MTP draft head) → 90 (probabilistic drafting + sampler fixes) → 107 (draft vocab from own outputs) → 114 (int4 output head) → 133 (DFlash2 block drafter) → **140.4** (same recipe, two newer years of card).

No magic wand. A stack of engineering steps, each buying another 6 to 43 percent, still compounding.

## What I didn't run

No long-context lanes yet, this boot is the 64k fast mode and the deep ladder needs the CTX=long reboot (128k, gives back some speed). No reproduction mode (his 3090 reads up to 381 tok/s there, DFLASH_TOKENS=15). No soak test, one evening of running. No wall power, GPU rail only. Maths probe is suspect, three models have now failed it identically-shaped.
